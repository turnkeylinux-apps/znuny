# Znuny asset provenance

The candidate deliberately does not reuse the upstream Znuny logo or any
commercial OTRS artwork. It also removes the historical product screenshots
from the inherited appliance because they show obsolete OTRS-branded user
interfaces and are not evidence of this Znuny v19 build.

The identical PNG stored at `.art/logo.png` and
`overlay/var/www/images/znuny.png` was created specifically for this migration
on 2026-08-27 under TurnKey release-engineering direction using OpenAI's
built-in image-generation tool. The prompt requested a neutral, text-only
rounded-square badge reading `ZNUNY`, explicitly excluding upstream logos,
trademark shapes, wordmarks, and watermarks. It is a TurnKey project asset,
not an upstream Znuny logo.

The badge and this appliance are not endorsed by the Znuny project or by the
vendor of current commercial OTRS. Future screenshots must be captured from a
validated TurnKey Znuny v19 instance, must not be copied from upstream
marketing material, and must have their capture or reuse provenance recorded
here before publication.
